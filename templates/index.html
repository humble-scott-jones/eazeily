Frequently Asked Questions

When is Eazeily launching?

We're working hard to launch in early 2025. Join our waitlist to get notified the moment we go live and be among the first to try it!

What will Eazeily cost?

We're keeping it simple and affordable for small businesses. Sign up to learn more about our plans and features.

What industries will you support?

We support realtors, restaurants, retail/boutiques, fitness studios, artisans, coaches/consultants, nonprofits, home services, healthcare providers, churches, and more. Each industry has custom templates and content styles.

Will I be able to customize the generated content?

Absolutely! All content is fully editable. Think of Eazeily as your creative starting point—you can tweak the tone, add details, or completely rewrite anything to make it perfectly yours.

What platforms will be supported?

Content will be optimized for Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, Twitter/X, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts. Each platform gets formatting tailored to its style and audience.

Will you post to social media for me?

Not initially, but it's on our roadmap! For now, Eazeily generates the content, and you copy/paste to your preferred scheduling tool or post directly. You maintain full control of when and where it goes live.
