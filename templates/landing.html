Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know.

Is it really free forever?
Yes! Our free plan gives you 25 content pieces per month forever. No credit card required. No hidden fees. No catches. We believe every small business deserves access to great content.
How is this different from ChatGPT?
ChatGPT doesn't know your business, your voice, or your goals. Eazeily learns your brand during a 3-minute setup and remembers everything. Every piece of content sounds like YOU, not a generic AI.
Do I need to be tech-savvy?
Not at all. If you can send a text message, you can use Eazeily. Just chat with it like you'd chat with a person. No prompts to memorize, no templates to fill out.
What if I don't like what it creates?
Just tell it what to change! "Make it shorter," "Add more emojis," "More professional tone"—it adjusts instantly. You're in full control.
Can I use this for multiple businesses?
Absolutely! Pro plans include 3 brand profiles, and Business plans have unlimited profiles. Switch between them instantly.
Will the content be unique, or will others have the same thing?
Every piece is 100% unique to your business. Eazeily creates custom content based on YOUR brand voice, YOUR goals, and YOUR specific request. No templates. No recycling.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. One-click cancellation. No hoops to jump through. No "are you sure?" emails. We're confident you'll love it, but if not, canceling is easy.
How does the 3-minute setup work?
Eazeily asks you a few conversational questions about your business, your voice, and your goals. That's it. No forms, no surveys—just a quick chat. Then you're ready to start creating.
