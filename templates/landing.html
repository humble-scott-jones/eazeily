Frequently asked questions

How does Swelly work?

Swelly asks you a few simple questions about your business, then generates a personalized content calendar with posts, reel scripts, and image suggestions. You can customize everything before posting to your social media accounts.

What industries does Swelly support?

We support realtors, restaurants, retail/boutiques, fitness studios, artisans, coaches/consultants, nonprofits, home services, healthcare providers, churches, and more. Each industry has custom templates and content styles.

Can I customize the generated content?

Absolutely! All content is fully editable. Think of Swelly as your creative starting point—you can tweak the tone, add details, or completely rewrite anything to make it perfectly yours.

Do you post to social media for me?

Not yet! Swelly generates the content, and you copy/paste to your preferred scheduling tool or post directly. We're focused on making great content—you maintain full control of when and where it goes live.

What platforms are supported?

Content is optimized for Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, Twitter/X, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts. Each platform gets formatting tailored to its style and audience.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes! There are no long-term contracts. You can cancel your subscription anytime from your account settings, and you'll retain access through the end of your billing period.

Is there a free trial?

Yes! You can try a 1-day content sample and 5 reel scripts completely free. Pro subscriptions include a 7-day free trial so you can explore all features risk-free.
